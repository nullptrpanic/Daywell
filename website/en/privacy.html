Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Daywell does not require an account. The app does not upload your settings, app selections, or workout progress to the developer's servers. It contains no advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

This policy explains how the app handles data, what happens when you contact support, and how this site is hosted.

1. Scope and Contact

This policy covers the Daywell app for iOS and iPadOS and this support site. Daywell is maintained by independent developer nullptrpanic. For privacy or data questions, email liyufeiat010@gmail.com.

2. Data Processed on Your Device

Daywell processes and stores data for the features you use on your device.

  • Scheduled blocks, repeat days, whether a block is enabled, Morning Mode settings, and temporary pass rules.
  • System identifiers for apps, categories, websites, and allowed apps selected through the system picker.
  • Active restrictions, recent blocks, and pass usage, so the app and its extensions can coordinate and track used passes.
  • Focus duration, system identifiers for recently selected focus apps, and the timer state of the current focus session.
  • Workout goals and sets you log, rest settings, and timer state.
  • Appearance preference, Screen Time authorization status, and state used to display errors and diagnose problems.

Settings and restriction state are stored in an on-device container shared by the app and its Screen Time extensions. Preferences such as appearance are stored in the system's local preferences. Daywell does not operate an account system, cloud sync, or remote data storage.

3. Screen Time Access

With your permission, Daywell uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings to select what to restrict, apply schedules, and receive a system callback when selected apps reach the Morning Mode usage allowance.

Selections are stored as system-provided identifiers. Daywell does not read messages, photos, credentials, or page content in selected apps or use selections for advertising or profiling.

You can revoke Screen Time access in system Settings. The corresponding restrictions will stop working, and you may need to select apps again after granting access.

4. Uploads, Sharing, and System Services

Daywell does not upload the on-device data above to the developer, sell user data, or use third-party advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking. The app does not access your contacts, location, or Apple Health data or measure your sleep.

The app may log monitoring registrations, restriction state, and errors to the device's system log for on-device diagnostics. It does not operate a channel for sending those logs to the developer.

iCloud or computer backups you enable may include app data. Apple provides backups, the App Store, TestFlight, and system diagnostics under its policies and your settings. You can manage those services in Apple's system settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Current settings, appearance preferences, and workout progress stay on your device. Daywell keeps recent block and pass records to avoid granting the same passes again after a restart or a time change. Ended block records more than about eight days old are cleared on a later refresh.

You can edit or remove blocks, change selections, and reset workout progress in the app. Deleting a block's configuration may leave an active restriction in place until its scheduled end. If the block is still listed, turn it off and confirm to end it early.

To remove all local app data, choose Delete App and remove its data in system settings. Offload App may retain documents and data. Manage copies in backups through Apple's backup settings. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your device.

6. Information You Send to Support

If you email the developer, we receive your email address, your message, and any screenshots or files you attach. We use those details to respond, investigate problems, and improve relevant features. We do not use them for advertising.

Support mail is sent and stored through an email provider. We keep it as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations, then delete it when it is no longer needed. You can email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent.

Cover unrelated private information before sending screenshots. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment details.

7. Support Site and External Links

This is a static site with no ads, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies set by the developer.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub processes requests and logs technical details such as visitors' IP addresses to provide and secure the service. This processing falls under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app does not upload your selections, schedules, or workout records when you visit the site.

Email links open your mail app. When you follow an external link, the other service's own privacy policy applies.

8. Policy Updates

When features or data handling change, we will update this policy and its date. If a new feature requires permission or consent, we will explain what it needs and ask when you use it.

Privacy Questions?
